앱 없이 — 아래 웹에서 삭제에서 Google 계정으로 로그인한 뒤 같은 절차를 진행합니다.

이메일 — 위 두 방법을 쓸 수 없으면 cgn3731@gmail.com 으로 요청해 주세요. 본인 확인 후 처리합니다.

Account Deletion

Dicekeep · com.fallman.dicekeep · Effective 2026-09-22

Deletion is permanent. Progress, collection, decks and records are removed and cannot be restored, even by signing in with the same Google account.

In the app — Shop → account section → 계정 삭제 (Delete account), then type DELETE to confirm. Without the app — use section 3 above. By email — cgn3731@gmail.com.

Deleted immediately: Google account link, profile, progression, collection, decks, records, shards, sessions and run records. Retained but anonymised: transaction records (order id, amount, date) for 5 years as required by Korean e-commerce law — the account link, purchase token and account reference are stripped, so the remaining rows cannot be traced to a person. Multiplayer match receipts expire automatically within 30 days. Guest progress stored only on your device is not held on our servers.
