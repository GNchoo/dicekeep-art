Both the portrait and landscape title paintings now show **five on the broad face and three on the narrow face**. The added center pip matches the existing two, while the castle, flags, sky, landscape, and screen layout remain the same.

The preserved `source-portrait.png` and `source-landscape.png` are edits of `tools/art-review/casual-pilot-2026-09-23/keyart/portrait-candidate-1.png` and `landscape-candidate-1.png`.
